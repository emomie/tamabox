Note: the form spans body+CTA. Since the CakePHP Form->create / Form->end braces the form, we need either (a) keep the button outside Form via `form="..."` attribute, or (b) reorder so Form->end() comes after the CTA. Since the form already has no `id`, simplest is to have the form open before body and close after CTA. The structure above does that — `Form->create` is inside the `tb-screen__body` and `Form->end` is below the `tb-screen__cta`. The submit button is logically inside the form even though it's visually positioned by CSS.

The submit `button` lives inside `.tb-screen__cta` and that div is between Form->create and Form->end so it works correctly.
